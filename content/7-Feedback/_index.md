### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**

The learning and working environment in the AWS First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program is very friendly and supportive. During the internship, I had the opportunity to communicate and collaborate with other members who shared the same interest in cloud computing and artificial intelligence.

The program created a professional environment where interns could actively learn, discuss technical issues, and exchange ideas during project development. However, I think organizing more team-building activities or knowledge-sharing sessions would help strengthen connections between participants.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**

The mentor and FCAJ organizing team provided valuable support throughout the internship. The learning materials, technical guidance, and project requirements were provided clearly, helping me understand the expected goals and development process.

When encountering technical difficulties related to AWS services, data processing, or system architecture, I received timely guidance and suggestions. I especially appreciated that the mentor encouraged interns to research and solve problems independently before providing solutions, which helped improve my self-learning ability.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**

The tasks during the internship were highly relevant to my Computer Science background. Through participating in the project **"Local Air Pollution Forecasting and Warning System using Machine Learning on AWS"**, I was able to apply knowledge related to databases, data processing, cloud computing, and system design.

Besides strengthening my existing knowledge, the internship also introduced me to new areas such as AWS cloud services, Data Lake architecture, and Machine Learning pipelines, which are valuable for my future career development.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**

The FCAJ program provided many opportunities to improve both technical and soft skills. During the internship, I learned how to work with AWS services such as Amazon S3, EC2, SageMaker, Kinesis Data Firehose, IAM, and CloudWatch.

In addition, I improved my skills in data processing, technical documentation, teamwork, problem-solving, and presenting technical solutions. These experiences helped me better understand how cloud-based systems are designed and operated in real-world projects.

**5. Program Culture & Team Spirit**

The working culture in the FCAJ program is open, collaborative, and supportive. Team members were willing to share knowledge and help each other when facing technical challenges.

Although the project was carried out through both offline and remote collaboration, team members actively communicated, discussed solutions, and coordinated tasks to ensure project progress. This helped me experience a professional teamwork environment similar to an actual technology project.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**

The FCAJ program provided a well-organized learning roadmap, detailed technical materials, and hands-on labs that allowed interns to practice AWS services directly.

The opportunity to access AWS resources and work on a practical project was a valuable benefit, helping me gain real experience with cloud technologies instead of only learning theoretical concepts.

---

### Additional Questions

**1. What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**

The most satisfying part of the internship was the opportunity to directly work with real AWS services and apply them to a practical project. Through building the Storage/Data component, I gained a clearer understanding of how data pipelines and cloud architectures operate in real-world environments.

**2. What do you think the program should improve for future interns?**

The program could provide more detailed guidance about project planning and expected technical outputs at the beginning stage. Additional technical workshops or sharing sessions about common implementation issues would also help interns adapt faster.

**3. If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they join this program? Why or why not?**

Yes. I would recommend the FCAJ program to students who are interested in cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and practical technology projects. The program provides valuable hands-on experience, useful learning materials, and a supportive environment for developing both technical and teamwork skills.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

* Provide more opportunities for interns to participate in technical discussions and knowledge-sharing sessions.

* Continue maintaining the FCAJ program in the future because it provides students with valuable opportunities to approach modern cloud technologies.

**Other comments:**

The FCAJ program was a meaningful experience that helped me understand the difference between academic knowledge and practical implementation. Through this internship, I developed a stronger interest in cloud computing and gained clearer direction for my future learning path.
